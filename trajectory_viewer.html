RGB-D and depth-only estimates in meters. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. All data is embedded; this viewer works offline.

These are local odometry estimates, without ground truth or loop closure. Agreement between methods does not prove accuracy. After tracking loss, each new segment starts at its own origin and cannot be joined to the previous segment.

Static comparison and tracking diagnostics

Axes follow the first depth optical frame: X right, Y down, Z forward. X–Z is not necessarily the floor plane because initial camera tilt is unknown.
